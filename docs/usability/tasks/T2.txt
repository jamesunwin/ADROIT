Task T2: what explains an excess

CDMS II's silicon detectors saw three events in their 2013 WIMP search (arXiv:1304.4279). Interpreted as elastic,
spin-independent, isospin-conserving dark matter scattering in the standard halo, at what dark matter mass and
dark matter-nucleon cross-section does the likelihood peak?

Answer: the mass in GeV and the cross-section in cm^2, and one sentence on how you obtained them.
