# Changelog

## 1.0.1

A corrective release. It repairs a spin-dependent direct-detection defect that strengthened some exclusions for models
coupling to both protons and neutrons (about 148–341 GeV in xenon), refuses calculations whose revised xenon nuclear
responses are missing or incomplete, and repairs further defects found by independent audits of the release: how
exclusions resting on an experimental record of unknown version are shown, the safety of a reassessment's new folder,
the halo assignments behind a joint reading, and the parameter-plane plots. What changed, who is affected and the
limits that remain are in [`docs/release_notes_v1.0.1.md`](docs/release_notes_v1.0.1.md).
